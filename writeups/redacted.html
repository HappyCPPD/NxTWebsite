REDACTED PDF redaction bypass

flag TISC{BRO!RedactPDFsProperlyLah!!!}

#Overview

Level 1 hands over a five-page PDF, TISC-26-091-SINGULARITY.pdf. Several sections of the document are covered with black rectangles, and the challenge description hints at redaction being the whole point.

#The bug

The PDF was never actually redacted. A black rectangle was drawn over the sensitive text, but the underlying text objects were left untouched underneath it - visually hidden, not removed. Anything that reads the PDF's text layer directly, instead of rendering it to a bitmap first, sees straight through the boxes.
