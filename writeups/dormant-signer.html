Dormant Signer ECDSA nonce reuse unmasks an escrow

#Artifact

  • chain/ - genesis.json, transactions.jsonl (the whole custody-desk history), replay.sh, HEAD
  • contracts/ - Authorizer.vy, VoucherDesk.vy, Notary.vy

Authorizer.vy pays withdrawals against a co-signature from an off-chain "automated signer". When the desk winds an authorizer down it files a recovery escrow and self-destructs:

where

  • running_digest = keccak256(concat(prev, keccak256(abi_encode(authorizer, request_id, amount, recipient)))) folded over every settled request - recomputable by replaying the chain;
  • escrow_mask(v,r,s) = keccak256(ESCROW_DOMAIN ++ r ++ s ++ v) over the signer's liveness signature - ecrecover(LIVENESS_DIGEST, v, r, s), i.e. only the signer can produce it.

#Method

  1. Recover the signer private key - ECDSA nonce reuse. The authorize co-signatures on the two authorizers leak the same r for two different messages (the RFC6979 nonce collided across deployments):

    recover.py → recovered signer keys, e.g. authorizer A c0ecef7be0815db43cf992c33dce2b92739576089c31e30ce0c30ac2e2919e52.

  2. Recompute the liveness signature deterministically (RFC6979, low-s, v ∈ {27,28}, raw LIVENESS_DIGEST, no message prefix) and derive mask = keccak(ESCROW_DOMAIN || r || s || v).

#Verification

  • Key recovery is confirmed by ecrecover over every authorize co-signature on the destroyed authorizer returning the recovered address (100% match - see final.py).
  • The unmasked escrow is ASCII and a well-formed hiCTF{…} token.

#Tooling notes

  • Authorizer filed masked_secret at storage slot 14; running_digest and settled follow it - read them with eth_getStorageAt.
  • Deterministic nonces (RFC6979) meant the "unrecoverable" key was actually leaked by a single r collision between the two authorizers.
