GIFTed 25 GIF frames tiled into a QR code

forensics
flag Skillbit{the_wrapping_was_more_interesting_than_the_gift}

#1. Recon

gifted.zip unpacks to a single file: gifted/gift.gif.

Its 82x82 size is tiny for a "gift" - the interesting part is the frame count.

#2. The trick - 25 frames are a 5x5 tile grid

The GIF is animated, 25 frames. 25 = 5x5. Each 82x82 frame is one tile of a larger 410x410 image (5*82 = 410). Paste every frame at (row, col) = (i//5, i%5) in frame order and the tiles line up: the three finder patterns of a QR code land exactly in the corners. That reassembled image is a QR code.
