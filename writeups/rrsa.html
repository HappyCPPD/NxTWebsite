#2. The observation - recover x+y for free

Expand n:

Therefore

Let A = (n-1)/r. Then A = x*y*r + (x+y), and taken mod r:

The primes sit near sqrt(n) ~ 3e57, while r ~ 1.3e39, so x, y ~ 2.2e18 and x + y < r. That means (x + y) % r is x + y exactly - no wrap. So:

  • s = A % r -> s = x + y
  • xy = (A - s) / r -> xy = x*y

x and y are the roots of z^2 - s*z + xy = 0, whose discriminant is the perfect square (x-y)^2. Recover them, rebuild p, q, and decrypt.
