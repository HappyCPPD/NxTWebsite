ChronoGrind Nine billion sequential squarings

crypto
flag hiCTF{n1c3_w0rk_n0w_t4k3_s0m3_r3$t}

Category: crypto / patience · Artifacts: extracted/chronogrind/state.dat (1358 B), flag.enc (68 B)

#Flag

Recovered after the full 9e9-step squaring chain (~158 min at ~950k squarings/s; the key came out as 96156b5d6965bf3ba35616cd280de13b2f7ae6e3734772083b7faf686cdf6148). The ChaCha20-Poly1305 tag verifies, which is cryptographic proof the key is the intended one - a wrong state fails the tag. Reproduce with python3 decrypt.py (reads grind_state.json, writes FLAG.txt).

#state.dat format (reversed)

  • magic CGD1, whole body XOR 0x4C; then a 2-byte header and a sequence of [tag u8][len u32 LE] TLV fields (tags 01 11 22 33 44 55 66 77 88).
  • Readable fields: team=testrun, and a note spelling out the scheme:
  • anchor/seed are 259-byte base-241 digit strings (2048 / 2045 bits); cycles = 9000000000. The gate sha256("anchor:seed:cycles")[:8] == rec[44] reproduces exactly (3afdd49ebe78a45a), confirming the parse.

#Why it's a grind

v = seed^(2^9e9) mod anchor, and anchor is a 2048-bit composite with no small factors, so the exponent cannot be reduced modulo λ(anchor) - the squaring chain has to be run literally, and it is inherently sequential (no CRT/parallel shortcut). Measured ~0.95-1.0 µs per modular squaring with gmpy2, i.e. ~2.6 h on one core - exactly the "bring a charger" line in the challenge.
