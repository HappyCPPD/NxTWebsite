Soul World LSB stego, two independent extractions

forensics
flag OWASP{s0ul_w0rld_h1d3s_1ts_s3cr3t}

#Challenge

A mysterious image from the Soul World contains a message hidden one bit at a time. Recover the concealed Soul Stone fragment.

  • Attachment: soul_world.png - 80×80, 8-bit RGB, non-interlaced PNG (19 KB)
  • Flag format: OWASP{...}

#Solution

The hint "hidden one bit at a time" points straight at LSB (least-significant bit) steganography. With three colour channels (R, G, B) per pixel and 80×80 pixels, the image holds 80 × 80 × 3 = 19200 bits = 2400 bytes of capacity - far more than a flag needs.

#Notes

  • No encryption, no compression, no interlace trickery - plain RGB LSB.
  • b1,rgb,lsb,xy is the canonical first thing to try on any PNG LSB challenge; if that fails, sweep bit depths (b2..b8), channel subsets (r/g/b/bgr), MSB vs LSB, and both xy/yx ordering (all covered by zsteg -a).
