Ghost in the Wire One byte per ICMP echo

forensics
flag hiCTF{1cmp_3xf1l_d3t3ct3d}

Category: network forensics · Artifact: extracted/ghost_traffic.pcap (30 packets)

#Hint

"…anomalous ICMP traffic … Something is being exfiltrated through the wire…"

#Solve

Follow the ICMP echo-request payloads. Packets 3-28 come from 10.13.37.1 and each carries one byte of exfiltrated data in its payload; the other ICMP traffic (192.168.1.100, which even pings server1 with 70696e672066726f6d2073657276657231 = "ping from server1") is noise.

Output: hiCTF{1cmp_3xf1l_d3t3ct3d} (the leading 1 is a leet "i" - the payload bytes really are 31 63 6d 70 = "1cmp").
