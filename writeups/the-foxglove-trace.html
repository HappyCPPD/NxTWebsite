The Foxglove Trace Rebuilding a lost Wi-Fi tracker's scoring

osint
flag hiCTF{FoxgloveCoffee_Guest}

Category: offline data analysis · Artifacts: notes.txt, aps.conf, run.log, scan_export.csv

#The scoring rule (reconstructed from the files)

notes.txt states the idea; run.log is the single good run of the (now lost) tracker.py and leaks the one number that was deliberately left out of the notes:

  • "moving" filter - from run.log for CafeDelMundo: step_delta=40 -> stationary, dropped but step_delta=41 -> moving, kept. Cutoff: keep only rows with step_delta >= 41.
  • per-AP score - sum over kept rows of signal * weight, where weight comes from aps.conf: FoxgloveCoffee_Guest=3, HomePlex_5G=2, CafeDelMundo=2, NETGEAR-8841=1. (Check: CafeDelMundo kept rows 58+60+64=182, x2 = 364, exactly the subtotal = 364 in run.log; HomePlex_5G kept row 87, x2 = 174 = the logged subtotal = 174.)
  • Only APs listed in aps.conf count; everything else in the export is ignored.

#Solve

The traps work exactly as advertised: HomePlex_5G is the loudest AP (signal 87-92) and also the most frequent (10 rows), but you are almost never moving there (1 kept row), so it finishes last. FoxgloveCoffee_Guest - the place the person keeps returning to - wins by a wide margin.
