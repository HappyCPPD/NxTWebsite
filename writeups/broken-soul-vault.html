Broken Soul Vault hex payload in a mislabelled .bin

#Description

The Soul Vault suffered metadata corruption during a recovery operation. Identify the valid recovered block and decode its contents.

#Attachments

  • metadata.bin.txt (87 bytes)
  • vault.log (133 bytes)

#Key takeaways

  • A .bin extension does not mean binary - check with file / xxd.
  • "Corrupted metadata + valid block" prompts usually mean: parse the container, validate each candidate against the flag format, and keep the one that fits.
  • Hex-dumping the guard values (00ff11aa, deadbeef) shows they are sentinels, not data - the only decodable payload is the middle line.
