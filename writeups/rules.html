#Rules worth remembering for the rest of the CTF

  • Flags are TFCCTF{...} unless a challenge says otherwise.
  • The Open division allows unrestricted AI use.
  • The Classic division allows only light AI assistance, no delegating the actual solving.
  • Both divisions share one prize scoreboard; the per-division boards are only for comparison within a division.
