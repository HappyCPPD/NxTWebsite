#Overview

the-collectors-trail.zip contains image_vault.zip (6 museum images + a catalogue colophon) and sealed_cabinet.zip (AES-encrypted, password unknown). Each of the 6 images hides one fragment xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx-cccc (8 raw bytes

  • SHA-256 first-2-bytes seal). Fragments and museum catalogue locators are ordered by the artists' birth years, then fed into HKDF to derive the cabinet password. The cabinet yields stego.png, whose HMAC-selected LSB payload points at a GitHub release; the annotated tag note supplies a serial used to AES-256-GCM decrypt the flag.

#Notes

  • The seam to watch: the Klimt image is a JPEG so steghide (not pixel LSB) applies; the passphrase is the full canonical locator, not just 912.
  • record-e2f1's data lives in the PNG filter byte stream (filter==2), not the pixel LSBs - a naive LSB scan finds nothing.
  • Two Belvedere works (Kiss 912, Death and the Maiden 3171) share a domain, so birth-year ordering is the only thing that disambiguates them in the digest.
