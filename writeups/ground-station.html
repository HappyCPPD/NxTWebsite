#1. Access derivation (reverse gsc-fw.bin)

gsc-fw.bin is a small stripped x86-64 ELF. Running it prints a POST banner and image-sig=de8c1297.

Recovered functions:

  • 0x40048e - key/vector expansion. LCG from seed 0x1f2e3d4c (s = s*0x19660d + 0x3c6ef35f), 16 iterations, byte = ((s >> 17) ^ (i*0x5b)) & 0xff. → KBUF = 50e5d52349171740d3823ef092cdb7bd
  • 0x400476 - rol32(value, n).
  • 0x4004ed/0x40053a - 32-bit load/store.
  • 0x400589 - the access routine: out8 = access(in16, key8). Loads 4 dwords a,b,c,d from in16 and 2 dwords e,f from key8, then 12 rounds:output = (a^c, b^d) packed LE = 8 bytes.

main (0x4006f8) calls access(KBUF, FIXVEC[:8]) where FIXVEC is the 16-byte .rodata value at 0x401280 = 4753432d37000123011b033b54000000 (the "fixed internal vector"), and prints the first 4 output bytes as image-sig. Our reconstruction reproduces de8c1297 exactly - the constant-verified anchor.

Live auth: the session NONCE is 8 bytes (16 hex). The access routine takes a 16-byte vector plus an 8-byte key - the nonce is the key, and the built-in vector KBUF is the input. So:
