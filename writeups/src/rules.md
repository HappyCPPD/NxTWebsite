# RULES

**Event:** TFC CTF 26
**Category:** misc
**Points:** 128
**Flag:** `TFCCTF{M4ny_ch4ng3s...m0r3_3ff0rt}`

---

## Overview

A baby misc challenge with no attachment. The title is the whole hint: the flag
is on the competition's own rules page.

## Solution

The rules page has a section on flag format. It states that flags normally look
like `TFCCTF{anything_goes_here}` and gives a worked example, and that example
string is itself the challenge flag:

```text
TFCCTF{M4ny_ch4ng3s...m0r3_3ff0rt}
```

Opening the rules page was enough to flip `RULES` to solved on the dashboard; the
account total moved from 229 points / 1 flag to 357 points / 2 flags.

## Rules worth remembering for the rest of the CTF

- Flags are `TFCCTF{...}` unless a challenge says otherwise.
- The Open division allows unrestricted AI use.
- The Classic division allows only light AI assistance, no delegating the actual
  solving.
- Both divisions share one prize scoreboard; the per-division boards are only for
  comparison within a division.
