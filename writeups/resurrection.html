#The one insight that solves it

Every recovered segment has the shape <ordinal-tag>--<leet-technique>_, e.g. 53c0nd--n7f5_runl157_cl5_g30m37ry_. The challenge note says "Each recovered phase reveals an ordinal identifier tag. Concatenate all five payload segments in order."

The ordinal tag is an ordering label, NOT flag bytes. It is stripped before concatenation - exactly the convention the author uses in the sibling challenge TESSERA (its labels 1ist-----, --s32nd--, iiiiiiiiiii-3hird--- are discarded, and its part-1 literal begins with the flag's own hiCTF{).

That explains the parts that look like anomalies:

  • part-1's decoded overlay contains a literal hiCTF{ → it is simply the flag opener, sitting right after the f!r5t-- label (author's house style).
  • part-5's payload ends in } → it is the flag closer.
  • part-3 decodes as th1jd… (j where the ordinal wants r) - irrelevant, the whole th1jd-- token is the label and is thrown away.

So: flag = hiCTF{ p1 p2 p3 p4 p5 }, where pi = segment i with its <ord>-- label removed.

The three challenge "silent spaces" map onto the five channels: Shattered-Geometry = PE-overlay (1) + NTFS runlist (2); Chrono-Echoes = TxR filetime (3) + evtx (4); Unmapped-Voids = $Bitmap orphans (5).

#Image quick facts

MBR + primary GPT + primary NTFS boot sector zeroed ("scorched"); the backup VBR (0x3FDFFE00) and backup GPT (0x3FFFFE00) survive. NTFS starts at 0x1000000. Work on work/fixed.raw (primary VBR restored from the backup) with fls/icat -o 32768. Only three non-zero regions: metadata/file area ~16 - 26 MB, $LogFile @0x15A88000, $MFT @0x15F55000, plus backup VBR/GPT. Many "files" (hives, kernel32/ntdll/svchost.dll, prefetch, audit_*.xlsx) are high-entropy decoys with fake magic.
